Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

App: Paylog (ペイログ)
Developer: Shibaken900
Last updated: 2026-05-24

1. Information We Collect

  • Spending records: App names, amounts, dates, categories entered by the user. Stored locally on your device only — never sent to external servers.
  • Purchase status: Pro plan subscription status via Apple App Store.
  • Advertising identifier: Google AdMob may collect IDFA for ad delivery (free-plan users only).

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing spending history display and management
  • Monthly budget and expense analysis
  • Displaying ads (free plan users only)
  • Anonymous usage analysis for improvements

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Ad delivery. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple In-App Purchase: Pro plan billing.
  • iTunes Search API (Apple Inc.): App icon fetching.

We do not sell or share your personal information with any other third parties.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

All spending records are stored locally on your device. Delete all data via Settings > Reset All Data.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated through app updates.
